# Steps 13 to 16: brief

Source brief for the /goal of steps 13 to 16, agreed with Julien on 2026-11-25 after a mobile-first best-practices search and a client audit. Julien chose to stabilise the existing product first: the events feature first planned as steps 16 to 19 is parked in [2026-11-25-events-idea.md](2026-11-25-events-idea.md). Each step still gets its own spec and plan; this file records what was decided before design.

## Step 13 · Mobile-first

- Bottom tab bar on mobile (Bibliothèque · Amis · Cadre · Compte) instead of the ☰ menu. Desktop unchanged.
- « Ajouter » in the thumb zone (tab bar or floating button: Claude decides).
- Touch targets of at least 44 px everywhere: a `pointer-coarse` variant in `ui/button.tsx` rather than case by case, plus the gaps found in the audit (chips, viewer, friends toggle, tiles, links, input).
- `viewport-fit=cover` and `env(safe-area-inset-*)` on the bottom bar, the bottom sheet and the frame. `theme-color` and `color-scheme` meta tags.
- Wake Lock on the frame, taken again when the page becomes visible.
- Viewer: a tap hides or shows the controls, a swipe keeps the current state. Arrows hidden on touch screens. Mouse: controls show on movement and hide after 3 s idle. Keyboard: arrows change photo, Tab brings the controls back.

## Step 14 · Photo upload

- Real progress (XHR `upload.onprogress`).
- Pending photo: an immediate greyed tile with progress, replaced by the real photo, removed on failure with a message.
- Multiple files (`multiple`), uploaded one after another.
- Downscale only when needed: long side over 4096 px or file over 10 MB → 4096 px, JPEG quality 0.92. Otherwise the file is sent untouched. No user setting. Documented guarantee: printable at 20×30 cm, 300 dpi. Reason: the server already refuses files over 10 MB, so 48–50 MP phone photos fail today.

## Step 15 · Mobile finish

- PWA manifest (installable, `display: standalone`, icons), Apple meta tags. No service worker.
- Skeleton loaders instead of « Chargement… ».
- Offline banner (`online`/`offline`) and a « Réessayer » button on failed loads.
- Keyboards: `enterKeyHint`, `autoCapitalize="none"` on e-mails, `inputMode` where useful.
- Drag down to close the bottom sheet and the viewer, hand-made on Radix (about 60 lines). Not vaul: its repository is marked unmaintained, and shadcn's Base UI drawer moved away from it.
- Pinch zoom and double-tap zoom in the viewer.
- Native share (`navigator.share`) of the invitation link, falling back to copy.
- Viewer: an intermediate Cloudinary size (`srcset`) instead of the original.
