# Library page refocused (Step 12) Design

Status: decided and built without the author, per « je veux que tu livres sans m'attendre ». The author reviews afterwards. Plan: [2026-11-25-library-page.md](../plans/2026-11-25-library-page.md).

## Request

The author, in French: « Ces trois cards doivent être complètement déplacée/refondu pour que la page bibliothèque soit vraiment dédié a la bibliothèque. La ca fait désordre. A toi de voir si tu pense qu'on devrait creer une page de dashboard avec des actions et informations principales qui aide a rediriger vers le reste mais en tout cas la vue amis propose deja l'ajout d'ami et la vue bibliothèque doit permettre de naviguer dans la bibliothèque, ajouter une photo, et gerer aussi les collections/album »

The three cards were « Ajouter une photo » (upload form), « Vos proches » (call to action towards the friends page) and « Mes collections » (collection list and actions), stacked above the photos.

## Decisions

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1  | **No dashboard page.** `/` stays the library.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | A dashboard would only redirect: the top bar (☰ on a phone) and the avatar menu already reach Amis, Invitations, Préférences and the frame. A page of shortcuts adds one tap before the photos, which are what the user opens the app for. |
| D2  | **« Vos proches » leaves the library.** The send panel's empty state keeps its link to the friends page (`NoFriends`, « Ajouter un ami »).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | The friends page already adds friends. The only place a missing friend blocks the user is the send panel, and it already says so with a link.                                                                                               |
| D3  | **Compact header**: « Bibliothèque », the photo count, « Ajouter » (primary), « Mode cadre », grid/list toggle. Actions wrap below the title at 360 px.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | One row instead of a card; the actions that act on the photos sit next to them.                                                                                                                                                             |
| D4  | **Photo count without a total from the API**: « N photos » once every page is loaded, « Plus de N photos » while pages remain, « Aucune photo » when empty. Inside a collection it counts that collection's photos.                                                                                                                                                                           | The list endpoint pages by cursor and has no total. Adding a `count` query for a label was not worth an API change.                                                                                                                         |
| D5  | **« Ajouter » opens a panel**: bottom sheet on a phone, centered dialog on a desktop, the same pattern as the send panel (extracted as `ResponsivePanel`). Two large buttons « Prendre une photo » (`capture="environment"`) and « Choisir une image », a preview, then « Ajouter à ma bibliothèque » or « Envoyer à un ami ».                                                                | Keeps the upload one tap away without a permanent form on the page. Two explicit buttons make the camera reachable on a phone, where a single file input hides it behind a system chooser.                                                  |
| D6  | **« Envoyer à un ami » closes the add panel and opens the send panel** with the chosen file.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Two stacked panels on a phone are confusing; the send panel already handles a device file.                                                                                                                                                  |
| D7  | **Empty library**: « Aucune photo pour l'instant. » and « Ajouter une photo », which opens the same panel.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | The first visit shows what to do next.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D8  | **Compact mail banner**: one row, « Vous avez N nouvelles photos », senders truncated, « Découvrir ».                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | It stays above the photos (family news first) without taking a card's height.                                                                                                                                                               |
| D9  | **Chip row** under the banner: « Toutes », each collection (alphabetical), « Nouvelle collection ». Only this row scrolls sideways; it bleeds to the screen edges so a cut chip reads as « more ». The selected chip scrolls into view.                                                                                                                                                       | Collections become the library's navigation instead of a card to manage.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| D10 | **The URL holds the collection**: each chip is a link to `/?collection=<id>`; back, reload and a saved link show the same collection. Switching pushes a history entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Matches how the viewer already uses `?photo=`.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| D11 | **Selected collection bar**: its name, then a ⋯ menu « Lire sur le cadre », « Renommer », « Supprimer », reusing step 10's `usePlayOnFrame`, `CollectionNameDialog` and `DeleteCollectionDialog`. The « Mes collections » card and `CollectionRow` are removed. Deleting the shown collection replaces the URL with `/`.                                                                      | Same actions, attached to what is shown. Replace, so back never returns to a deleted collection.                                                                                                                                            |
| D12 | **« Nouvelle collection » chip** opens the create dialog, then navigates to the new (empty) collection.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The empty-collection notice then tells how to fill it.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D13 | **Server filter** `GET /api/photos?collectionId=<id>`: the repository reads the collection's `photoIds` with an owner check, then pages with `id: { lt: cursor, in: photoIds }`. Unknown, malformed or other account's id → `404 COLLECTION_NOT_FOUND`; a repeated parameter → `400 QUERY_INVALID`. Same order and cursor as the whole library.                                               | No schema change; the owner check never reveals that someone else's collection exists.                                                                                                                                                      |
| D14 | **Library hook filters by collection**: `usePhotoLibrary(collectionId)` resets its list during render when the collection changes (no keyed remount, so the mail banner keeps its socket), passes the filter to every page, and reloads a collection on `collections:changed` (a photo enters it only by being filed). New photos (`photo:new`, `photo:opened`) are added only in « Toutes ». | One hook for both views; the viewer, which reads the same list, browses only the photos shown.                                                                                                                                              |
| D15 | **Viewer keeps the collection**: `?photo=` is set or removed on a copy of the current search params.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Closing a photo returns to the collection; swiping stays inside it.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| D16 | **Unavailable collection**: deleted elsewhere, unknown or mistyped → « Cette collection n'existe plus. »; collections list failed → « Cette collection n'a pas pu être chargée. »; both with « Voir toutes les photos ». The viewer does not open over them.                                                                                                                                  | A dead link explains itself and offers the way back.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| D17 | **Empty collection**: « Cette collection est vide. », how to file photos, and « Ajouter une photo ».                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D18 | **A photo added while a collection is shown is filed into it** (by id, even while the collections list loads), with a toast « Photo ajoutée à « [nom] ». ».                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | The user picked the collection first: that is where the photo was wanted. Otherwise it would vanish from the view it was added from.                                                                                                        |
| D19 | **44 px touch targets** on coarse pointers for every new control (`pointer-coarse:h-11` / `size-11`); the grid/list toggle gets it through `*:pointer-coarse:size-11`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Project rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

## Rejected

- **Dashboard page** (D1).
- **Keyed remount of the library per collection**: simpler reset, but it would remount the mail banner and its socket on every chip tap.
- **A `count` in the photos API** (D4).
- **Showing each collection's photo count on its chip**: chips stay short; the header counts the shown collection.

## Open questions (default applied)

1. **Cancelling the send panel after « Envoyer à un ami » abandons the chosen photo** (R-PAR-22). Default: abandon, as closing the add panel does (R-BIB-44). Alternative: reopen the add panel with the file.
2. **Leaving the frame started from a photo of a collection returns to `/?photo=<id>` without the collection**: the viewer then walks the whole library. Default: accepted; carrying the search params through the frame's history state is a later change.
3. **The count label without a total** (D4): « Plus de 30 photos » until the last page. Alternative: a `count` endpoint.
4. **Collections list failed to load**: a `/?collection=` link shows « Cette collection n'a pas pu être chargée. » even though the photos endpoint could answer. Default: the notice, since the name and the menu are unknown.
5. **Naming « Toutes »** for the whole library chip. Alternative: « Toutes les photos » (longer on a 360 px row).
6. **Focus after deleting the shown collection** falls to the page: the ⋯ button leaves with the navigation to `/`.
