# Design tokens and the « Encre » palette Design

Status: decided by the author (palette, default, switching model, frame accent), built without them; the author reviews afterwards. Plan: [2026-11-25-design-tokens.md](../plans/2026-11-25-design-tokens.md).

## Request

- A new palette, **« Encre »**: a slate blue primary on slightly warm paper neutrals. It becomes the **default**. Today's shadcn neutral palette stays available as **« Actuelle »**.
- Switching palette is **one attribute on `<html>`**: `data-palette="encre"` or `"actuelle"`, independent of light/dark (the `.dark` class `theme.ts` puts on `<html>`). 2 palettes × 2 modes.
- The frame accent used in the app (« Mode cadre », the current-photo rings, the mail card) **follows the palette**. « Actuelle » keeps today's exact values.
- **No hard-coded colour in components**: semantic tokens only.
- The person chooses the palette next to the theme, and the choice is remembered like the theme.

## Current behaviour (audit, 01c88bf)

- `client/src/index.css` holds the shadcn neutral tokens in `:root` and `.dark`, plus four frame tokens (`--frame-background`, `--frame-accent`, `--frame-mat`, `--frame-print`) that never change with the mode.
- The `--sidebar-*` and `--chart-*` tokens (and their `@theme inline` entries) are not used by any component.
- Components hard-code colours in about thirty places: the photo chrome (`bg-black/45…75`, `text-white`, `text-white/80`, `bg-white/10…25`, `ring-white/10`, the caption gradients `from-black/60…75`), the frame (`from-white/6` wall glow, `ring-white/90` bevel, `shadow-black/60`, `text-black/75` on the print), `text-black` on the accent and print pills, `text-white` on the destructive count badge, and three modal backdrops that disagree (`bg-black/10` with a blur on dialog and alert-dialog, `bg-black/50` without blur on the sheet).
- `client/index.html` hard-codes `theme-color` (`#ffffff` light, `#0a0a0a` dark, by media query): the browser bar follows the device, not the theme chosen in the app (R-APP-57).
- The mail card draws its icon in `text-frame-accent` on the card: 1.75:1 in light, invisible-ish.

## Decisions

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| D1  | **One block of semantic values per palette and mode**, not a primitive layer plus a semantic layer. `:root` and `.dark` hold « Encre »; `:root[data-palette="actuelle"]` and `:root[data-palette="actuelle"].dark, :root[data-palette="actuelle"] .dark` hold « Actuelle ». Each block sets every shadcn name (`--background`, `--primary`, `--muted`, `--border`, `--ring`…) plus `--destructive-foreground`, `--frame-accent` and `--frame-accent-foreground`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | A primitive layer would still need four sets of values (two palettes × two modes, since dark maps `--primary` to a lighter blue than light does), and the semantic layer on top would only rename them (`--primary: var(--encre-primary)`): twice the names, one more hop to explain, no reuse, because the two palettes share no structure (« Actuelle » is pure grey with alpha borders in dark). The hard requirement holds: components only use semantic tokens, switching is one attribute. |
| D2  | **« Encre » is written on `:root` / `.dark` without the attribute.** A page without `data-palette` (or with an unknown value) gets « Encre ». `client/index.html` still sets `data-palette="encre"`, so the markup says what the page shows.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The fallback comes from the CSS itself, no JavaScript needed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| D3  | **« Actuelle » wins by specificity, and its dark block also matches a nested `.dark`.** `:root[data-palette="actuelle"]` (0,2,0) beats `:root` / `.dark` (0,1,0); the dark block (0,3,0) beats the light one. The `:root[data-palette="actuelle"] .dark` selector covers the forced-dark scopes (frame page, viewer, mail reveal, the frame menus and dialogs that portal to `body` with a `dark` class); « Encre »'s plain `.dark` already matches them. Both palettes' dark blocks declare the same names as their light blocks, so nothing from light leaks into a dark scope.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Without the descendant selector, the frame shown under « Actuelle » would mix « Encre » dark values into it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| D4  | **Constant tokens, declared once in `:root`, never redeclared**: they are inherited by every scope whatever the palette or mode. Photo chrome: `--overlay` (black 55 %, the dark pill on a photo), `--overlay-strong` (black 75 %, that pill hovered or open), `--overlay-hover` (white 10 %, a bare button hovered on a photo), `--overlay-selected` (white 20 %, a pressed button, and the upload progress track), `--overlay-border` (white 15 %, the glass edge), `--on-overlay` (white), `--on-overlay-muted` (white 80 %), `--scrim` (black 70 %, where a caption gradient starts). Frame: `--frame-wall-glow` (white 6 %), `--frame-bevel` (white 90 %), `--frame-shadow` (black 60 %, also the reveal dialog's print), `--frame-print-foreground` (a warm dark grey, 0.33 0.01 60). App: `--backdrop` (black 30 %). `--frame-background`, `--frame-mat` and `--frame-print` stay constant too. | The photo chrome sits on a photo, not on the page: it must stay dark and neutral under any palette. The frame is an object on a wall (dark wall, cardboard mat, paper print): a palette tint would make it look like an app screen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| D5  | **Value merges** to keep the list lean: the glass control (`bg-black/45`) and the tile/upload pills (`/55`) share `--overlay`; their hovers (`/65`, `/75`) share `--overlay-strong`; the three caption gradients (`from-black/60`, `/70`, `/75` with `via-black/30`) share `--scrim` (the frame's `via` stop is dropped: a linear 70 % → 0 gradient is 35 % halfway, the old curve was 30 %); the pressed states (`white/15`, `/20`) and the upload track (`white/25`) share `--overlay-selected`; the glass ring goes from `white/10` to `white/15` (`--overlay-border`, distinct from the 10 % hover); the waiting pill's `text-black` and the print's `text-black/75` share `--frame-print-foreground`; the arrivals pill dot (`bg-black/70`) uses `--frame-accent-foreground`.                                                                                                                     | Small visual shifts (5 to 10 % of alpha) in exchange for one name per role.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D6  | **One modal backdrop, `--backdrop` = black 30 %**, for dialog, alert-dialog and sheet; the dialogs keep their small blur, the sheet stays without.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | 10 % barely separated the dialog from the page without the blur (browsers without `backdrop-filter`), 50 % turned the page black behind a side sheet. 30 % reads in both modes with or without the blur.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| D7  | **Frame accent per palette.** « Encre »: `--frame-accent` 0.82 0.1 78 (light) / 0.8 0.09 78 (dark), with `--frame-accent-foreground` 0.24 0.03 60 / 0.22 0.03 60: a softer brass that sits with the slate blue. « Actuelle »: 0.82 0.12 75 in both modes with black text, today's exact look. The frame (always dark) takes the dark value.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | The author's decision: the accent belongs to the palette. A foreground token replaces `text-black` on the accent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| D8  | **The mail card's icon gets a chip**: `bg-frame-accent` with `text-frame-accent-foreground`, round, the border stays `border-frame-accent/60`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | The accent as text or icon on a light card is 1.75:1. On its own background the foreground reaches 9:1, in every palette and mode, without a third « darker accent » token.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| D9  | **`--destructive-foreground`**, for the friends request count badge (`text-white` before). « Encre »: paper on 0.55 0.2 27 (light), a dark red-brown 0.2 0.03 25 on 0.7 0.17 25 (dark). « Actuelle »: white in both modes (today's look).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | White on the light dark-mode red is 2.77:1. « Actuelle » keeps it as it was (open point).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| D10 | **`success` is not added.** No component shows a success colour today (toasts use the popover colours, the check icon inherits the text).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A token without a consumer is dead weight; the approved value (0.52 0.12 152 / 0.74 0.12 152, 4.98:1 and 8.56:1 on the backgrounds) is recorded here for the day a component needs it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| D11 | **The browser bar follows the theme and the palette.** `themeColor.ts` reads the computed `--background` of `<html>` and writes it in both `theme-color` metas; `theme.ts` (`applyTheme`) and `palette.ts` (`applyPalette`) call it after changing the class or the attribute. `index.html` keeps the two media metas with « Encre »'s background (`#fcfaf7`, `#0f1215`) for the first paint, before the script runs. Replaces R-APP-57.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | One source of truth, the CSS token; no colour table duplicated in TypeScript. Both metas carry the same value once the script runs, so the media query no longer matters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| D12 | **Palette choice stored like the theme**: `palette.ts`, `localStorage` key `familyframe-palette`, `Palette = 'encre' \| 'actuelle'`, default `encre`, applied at startup in `main.tsx` (`initPalette`) before `initTheme`. A blocked storage keeps the choice for the visit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Same model as `theme.ts`, so the code reads the same; independent of the theme as decided.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D13 | **The switch lives in the account menu, under « Thème »**: a « Palette » row (`PaletteToggle`) with two text items « Encre » and « Actuelle », built like `ThemeToggle`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The theme toggle lives there (`UserMenu`); the settings page cards (`SettingToggleGroup`) are frame settings saved to the server, this is a local appearance choice.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| D14 | **`--sidebar-*` and `--chart-*` are removed**, in their own commit, before the palette blocks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Unused; without them each palette block is half as long. The shadcn CLI writes them back if a sidebar or chart component is added one day.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D15 | **`@theme inline` keeps `var()` references only**, with the new colour tokens added (`--color-overlay: var(--overlay)`…).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Tailwind reads the variable at use time, so a utility follows the palette.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| D16 | **`preparePhoto.ts`'s `fillStyle = 'white'` stays**: it is the canvas fill behind a transparent image before JPEG encoding, mirrored by the server, not a UI colour.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Not a theme concern.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

## Open points

- `--border` (1.29:1 light, both palettes) is under the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11 for input outlines, as shadcn's neutral is; unchanged.
- The current-photo ring (`ring-frame-accent`) on the light library page is about 1.75:1 (UI 3:1 not met), in both palettes; kept as decided (the ring is paired with `aria-current`).
- « Actuelle » keeps white on the dark-mode destructive (2.77:1), today's look.
