# Photo upload (Step 14) Design

Status: decided and built without the author, per « je veux que tu livres sans m'attendre ». The author reviews afterwards. Plan: [2026-11-25-photo-upload.md](../plans/2026-11-25-photo-upload.md). Brief: [2026-11-25-steps-13-16-brief.md](2026-11-25-steps-13-16-brief.md), « Step 14 ». Built in parallel with step 13 and rebased on it (`feat/library-surface`, bd9e332, then its final tip 2af118f): D3 and D18 follow step 13's library surface and floating « Ajouter ».

## Request

From the brief agreed with the author: real progress (XHR `upload.onprogress`); a pending photo, an immediate greyed tile with progress, replaced by the real photo, removed on failure with a message; multiple files uploaded one after another; downscale only when needed (long side over 4096 px or file over 10 MB → 4096 px, JPEG quality 0.92, otherwise the file is sent untouched, no user setting), with the documented guarantee « printable at 20×30 cm, 300 dpi ». Reason: the server refuses files over 10 MB, so 48–50 MP phone photos fail today.

The controller fixed the main choices (XHR helper, pending tile behaviour, sequential queue, one summary toast, a maximum per selection, friend sending, pure size rule, no server change); the details below are this design's.

## Rejected

- **Parallel uploads**: faster on fibre, but on a phone every progress bar crawls and the first photo lands later.
- **Optimistic insert into `photos`** with a temporary id: the viewer, the ⋯ menu, the count label and the socket merge all assume server photos.
- **Cancel button on a pending tile**: not asked; an aborted XHR already resolves `null`, so it can come later.
- **Downscale every photo to a fixed size**: loses detail and EXIF for photos that fit.
- **Keeping the first 20 of a larger selection**: silent cut.

## Open questions (default applied)

1. **The 20×30 guarantee is about the client step only.** The server still re-encodes every photo to 1920 px on its long side (R-BIB-08) and keeps only that copy, about 16 cm at 300 dpi. The product doc says the reduction before sending keeps enough detail for a 20×30 print and that the photo kept stays the display copy. Default: documented as is. Making the stored photo printable means keeping a larger original on Cloudinary, a server and storage change for a later step.
2. **Label**: resolved, « Choisir des images » (review fix 1). It touches the long `/` row of ARCHITECTURE's pages table, which step 13 also edits: expect a conflict there at the rebase.
3. **In a collection, the photo appeared a moment after its tile left**: resolved, the tile now waits for the filing (D7, review fix 3). A short gap may remain until the `collections:changed` reload brings the photo.
4. **A reduced photo is a new file**: the duplicate check (R-BIB-43) compares bytes. Sending the same large photo twice from the same browser gives the same bytes and is refused; from another browser it may not be. Default: accepted.
5. **Reload or closing the app drops queued uploads** (R-BIB-58). Default: accepted, nothing persists the files.
6. **Decoding cost**: every photo is decoded once to read its size, even when it then goes untouched (about 100–300 ms for 12 MP on a phone, shown as « 0 % »). Default: accepted at review (won't fix 13); reading the size from the file header would need a parser.
7. **`toReversed()`** (ES2023, Safari 16+) orders the pending tiles. Default: kept at review (won't fix 12), the client already relies on ES2023-era APIs.
8. **Maximum per choice** (D11): 20. Alternative: 10 or 50.
