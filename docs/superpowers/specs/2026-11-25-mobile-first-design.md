# Mobile-first (Step 13) Design

Status: decided and built without the author, per « je veux que tu livres sans m'attendre ». The author reviews afterwards. Source brief: [2026-11-25-steps-13-16-brief.md](2026-11-25-steps-13-16-brief.md), section « Step 13 ». Plan: [2026-11-25-mobile-first.md](../plans/2026-11-25-mobile-first.md).

## Request

From the brief, refined by the controller's binding decisions:

- A bottom tab bar on phones (Bibliothèque · Amis · Cadre · Compte) replaces the ☰ menu; desktop unchanged.
- « Ajouter » in the thumb zone.
- Touch targets of at least 44 px on coarse pointers, through the shared `ui/button.tsx` sizes rather than case by case.
- `viewport-fit=cover`, `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, `theme-color` and `color-scheme` metas.
- Wake Lock on the frame.
- Viewer chrome: a tap toggles the controls, a swipe keeps them; arrows hidden on touch; the mouse shows them on movement and they hide after 3 s idle; Tab brings them back.

## Current behaviour (audit, d58d3b8)

- `client/index.html` has a bare viewport meta; no `env(safe-area-inset-*)` anywhere.
- Navigation: a sticky top `AccountBar` (logo, `DesktopNav` from `md`, avatar `UserMenu` from `md`) and, below `md`, a ☰ `MobileNav` opening a left sheet with the user badge, the nav items (Ma bibliothèque, Cadre, Amis, Invitations for the admin), Préférences, the theme toggle and « Se déconnecter ».
- « Ajouter » and « Mode cadre » sit in the library header.
- `ui/button.tsx` sizes have no coarse override (`default` h-8, `sm` h-7, `xs` h-6, `lg` h-9, `icon` 32 px, `icon-sm` 28 px, `icon-lg` 36 px): each component added `pointer-coarse:h-11` / `size-11` itself, and several did not (the ☰ is 32 px, `ui/input.tsx` is h-8, the toggles are h-7 to h-9, the password eye is 36 px wide, the menu items are about 28 px high).
- No Wake Lock: a phone or a tablet used as a frame goes to sleep.
- The viewer's ‹ › arrows sit over the photo on a phone; its controls never hide.

## Rejected

- **Keeping the ☰ menu next to the tabs**: two navigations for the same four places.
- **Moving logout and the theme to the settings page**: duplicates the avatar menu, which the desktop keeps anyway.
- **A « + » tab in the middle of the bar**: mixes an action with places, and is not reachable from the other pages' context.
- **Per-component `pointer-coarse:` classes** (the step 12 way): already missed several controls.
- **An inline script in `index.html` to apply the saved theme before the first paint**: one more piece of code outside the app for an edge case (a theme chosen against the device); see Q1.
- **Sharing the viewer chrome hook with the frame**: D14.
- **A client test runner for the idle timer and the tap rule**: the client has none, and adding one is a new dependency.

## Open questions (default applied)

1. **Theme chosen against the device**: the browser bar keeps the device's colour, and the first paint may flash the device's colours before the script applies the theme. Default: accepted (R-APP-57). Alternative: an inline script and a `theme-color` updated by `theme.ts`.
2. **No tab is active on `/admin/invitations`**, reached from the avatar menu. Default: none. Alternative: mark « Compte » active there.
3. **The « Compte » tab opens a page titled « Préférences »**. Default: kept, as the controller asked. Alternative: rename the page « Compte ».
4. **Toasts are raised above the tab bar on every phone page**, the frame and the login page included, where there is no bar. Default: accepted; the offset is one constant.
5. **A refused Wake Lock is not logged** (D13). Alternative: a `console.warn` for whoever debugs a sleeping frame.
6. **The frame keeps its 4 s rule and a tap that only shows the controls** (D14). Alternative: the viewer's tap toggle on the frame too.
7. **On touch the viewer controls never hide by themselves** (D15). Alternative: hide after a few seconds as with a mouse.
8. **The desktop « Cadre » link does not pass the library state** (unchanged, desktop is out of scope), unlike the tab on the library page.
9. **Page content of a sideways phone wider than `md`** gets the desktop layout (no tab bar), with side insets only.
10. **The hidden caption stays readable to screen readers** (it is faded, not `inert`: it holds nothing to focus). Alternative: `inert` like the controls, so a screen reader reads only what is visible.
11. **← → change photo without showing the controls** (R-BIB-51); only Tab and the mouse show them. Alternative: any key shows them, as on the frame.
