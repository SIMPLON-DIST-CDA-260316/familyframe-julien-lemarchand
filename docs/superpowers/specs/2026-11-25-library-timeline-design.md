# Library timeline (after step 13) Design

Status: decided and built without the author, per « je veux que tu livres sans m'attendre ». The author reviews afterwards. Asked by the controller after step 13's library surface, with Google Photos as the model: take what is good, do not copy it. Plan: [2026-11-25-library-timeline.md](../plans/2026-11-25-library-timeline.md). Branch `feat/library-timeline`, on top of `feat/library-surface`.

## Request

From the controller, refined after browsing Google Photos:

- **Date groups**: the photos grouped by day, newest first, under a small bold label: « Aujourd'hui », « Hier », then « dim. 22 nov. », with the year when it is not the current one. They must work with « Charger plus » (no repeated header) and leave room for step 14's pending tiles at the front of the first group. One group per line: Google's two short days side by side is too clever for us.
- **Collection cards** in place of the chips: a cover (the newest photo), the name and « N éléments » under the image (Google Albums), « Toutes » as the selected state, a « Nouvelle collection » card, about 150 px on phones and 180 px on desktop, a horizontal scroll with snap, edge-aligned, no arrow button, the chips' keyboard and aria behaviour, a neutral placeholder for an empty collection.
- **Collection page header**: with `?collection=` the header becomes the collection's own: its name as a large title, « N photos · 1 nov. 2022 – 22 nov. 2026 », and a « … » menu with what already exists for a collection. « Toutes » keeps « Bibliothèque ».

## Current behaviour (audit, 2af118f)

- `LibrarySection` hands `library.photos` (every loaded page, newest first by id, R-BIB-20) to `PhotoGrid` or `PhotoList`; list rows show « 24 nov. 2026, 14:05 » in local time (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`, `createdAt`).
- `CollectionChips`: a scrolling `nav` of `Link` chips (`aria-current="page"`, the selected one scrolled into view, `-mx-4 px-4` to the screen edges), « Toutes » first, « Nouvelle collection » last.
- `SelectedCollectionBar`, under the chips: the collection's name as an `h2` and its « … » menu (« Lire sur le cadre », « Renommer », « Supprimer »). The header always says « Bibliothèque » with the loaded count.
- `GET /api/collections` answers `{ id, name, photoIds }`; `photoIds` already gives the count. No cover, no dates: the service keeps live photo ids only (`findLivePhotoIds`).

## Decisions

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Why                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| T1  | **Group by the device's calendar day** of `createdAt` (`photoDays.ts`, `groupByDay(photos, now)`), the field and time zone the list already used. Labels: « Aujourd'hui », « Hier », else `Intl.DateTimeFormat('fr-FR', { weekday: 'short', day: 'numeric', month: 'short' })` (« dim. 22 nov. »), with `year: 'numeric'` when the year differs from now's.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A photo added at 23:30 belongs to that evening for its viewer, not to the next UTC day. `Intl` gives the French abbreviations for free.                                         |
| T2  | **Groups keyed by day in a `Map`**, built from every loaded photo at each render. A day continued on the next « Charger plus » page joins its group, so its header never repeats; a photo out of order (a received photo entering at its reception date) still joins its own day.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Rebuilding is cheap (a few hundred photos) and needs no state. Grouping by neighbours would repeat a header whenever the order is not strictly by day.                          |
| T3  | **`PhotoGrid` and `PhotoList` take `days: PhotoDay[]`**; each day is a `DateGroup` (`section` labelled by an `h2`, `text-sm font-semibold`) with its own grid or list, 24 px between days. Step 14's pending tiles can be prepended to the first day's photos.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Headings let screen readers jump from day to day. A grid per day starts each day on a new row, like the photo apps.                                                             |
| T4  | **The list gets the same headers**, and its rows show the time only (« 14:05 »); the image's `alt` keeps the full date for screen readers. R-BIB-23 amended.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | It stayed simple: the same `DateGroup`. The date beside each row would repeat its header.                                                                                       |
| T5  | **`now` is read at render.** A page left open past midnight keeps « Aujourd'hui » for the day before until anything re-renders the library (a photo arriving, « Charger plus », a reload). Written in R-BIB-54.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | A midnight timer would be code to explain for a rare case.                                                                                                                      |
| T6  | **The API gives each collection `coverUrl`, `oldestPhotoAt`, `newestPhotoAt`** (all `null` while empty). The cover is the signed square thumbnail of its newest photo by `createdAt`, whatever the order of adding: the library's own transformation (`thumbnailUrl` in `lib/cloudinary.ts`), already allowed under strict transformations. The dates are its oldest and newest photos'. They come from the query that already kept the live photos, now `findLivePhotos` with `cloudinaryId` and `createdAt`: no extra request.                              | The client cannot build a signed URL, and the collection's photos are not loaded on the library page. The date range costs no request, so it is justified for the header (T10). |
| T7  | **The cover rule is a pure function, `photoSpan(photos, thumbnailUrl)`**, and the service takes `thumbnailUrl` as a dependency (`app.ts` passes the real one): unit tested without Cloudinary (`collections.service.cover.test.ts`). Bruno asserts the empty shape at creation, the one-photo shape after an add (cover equals the photo's `thumbnailUrl`, signed; both dates equal its `createdAt`) and the empty shape after removal.                                                                                                                       | AGENTS.md: a service rule changes with its unit test, an endpoint with its Bruno requests.                                                                                      |
| T8  | **Cards** (`CollectionsRow`, `CollectionCard`, `CardText`): a `Link` per collection, a square cover with the name (`truncate`, full name in `title`) and « N photos » (`photoCountLabel`, shared with the header) under it; `w-[150px]`, `sm:w-[180px]`. « Toutes » has an `ImagesIcon` cover and no count (the library has no total, R-BIB-21); « Nouvelle collection » is a button card with a « + ». An empty collection shows a `FolderIcon` on `bg-muted`. The cover image has `alt=""`: the name in the card names the link.                            | Name under the image, per the controller: readable on any photo and in the light theme, no gradient.                                                                            |
| T9  | **The row keeps the chips' behaviour** (`nav` « Collections », links with `aria-current="page"`, the selected card scrolled into view, `-mx-4 px-4` to the screen edges) and adds `snap-x snap-mandatory` with `snap-start` items and `scroll-px-4`, so a card stops on the header's edge. The selected card's cover gets `ring-3 ring-primary`; the focus ring sits on the cover too (`group-focus-visible`). Scroll only, no arrow button.                                                                                                                  | On a 360 px screen two cards and a cut third say « more ».                                                                                                                      |
| T10 | **A shown collection titles the page** (`CollectionTitle`, was `SelectedCollectionBar`): its name as the `h1` (`text-2xl`, `sm:text-3xl`), its existing « … » menu beside it, and `collectionSummary`: « 12 photos · 1 nov. 2022 – 22 nov. 2026 », one date when both fall on the same day, the count alone while empty. `LibraryHeader` takes its title as an element; « Toutes », a collection still loading, missing or failed keep `LibraryTitle` (« Bibliothèque »). The menu offers nothing new: « Lire sur le cadre » already sets the frame's source. | The collection was a second, smaller heading under the chips; now the page says where you are.                                                                                  |
| T11 | **Both menus are `w-max`**: Radix's content takes its trigger's width (with a 128 px floor), so under a small « … » items wrapped. The photo menu got it on `feat/library-surface` (c74b15b), the collection menu here.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Found by the controller in Chrome at 360 px.                                                                                                                                    |

## Open questions

1. **Bruno was not run in the throwaway clone**: it has no `server/.env` (gitignored), so the API cannot start, and using the real repository's secrets is out of bounds. The requests are written; `pnpm test:bruno` against an API on another port (`PORT=3001`) is to be run where the secrets are.
2. **« Toutes » has an icon, not a photo**: its newest photo is only known once the library is loaded, and a cover that changes with the selected collection's photos would mislead. Alternative: the library's first photo when « Toutes » is shown.
3. **While collections load** on a `?collection=` address, the header says « Bibliothèque » for a moment before the collection's name.
4. **Out of scope, noted for later** (controller): multi-selection (check on hover, long press, a whole day; the author finds check-on-hover unintuitive); the « Souvenirs » carousel; a search bar.
