# Step 11: auto selections

- Date: 2026-11-25
- Scope: step 11 « Sélections automatiques ». The frame plays a day's selection (N photos drawn at random, the same lot all day, a new lot every day), and a « radio » started from any photo: a suite built on context (close dates, same sender, same collections), topped up at random. Server (two read-only endpoints, Bruno) and client. **No new dependency, no schema change, Prisma stays on v6.**

## Goal

A grandparent's frame plays the whole library, hundreds of photos, always newest first. Two ways to see something else without curating anything:

- « Sélection du jour » on the living-room frame: twenty photos a day, a surprise each morning, the same twenty on the kitchen tablet.
- « Lancer la radio » on a photo of the beach: the frame follows with the other beach photos, the ones the same cousin sent that week, those of the « Vacances » collection, then a few others at random.

## Brief (from the goal)

- « Sélection du jour : N photos tirées au hasard, le même lot toute la journée, un nouveau lot chaque jour, jouable en mode cadre. »
- « "Radio" à partir d'une photo : une suite construite sur le contexte (dates proches, même expéditeur, mêmes collections), complétée au hasard. »

## Rejected

- **Storing each day's lot** (a `DailySelection` model): a schema change and a `db:push` for something a hash gives for free.
- **A seeded PRNG for the daily draw**: the hash rank is simpler to explain and stable when a photo is added or deleted (others keep their rank).
- **Re-drawing the whole lot whenever the library changes**: a photo added at noon would replace morning photos; hence older photos first.
- **Sending full photos in `Selection`**: duplicates the feed; an unopened photo would need its own filtering.
- **Storing the radio as a frame setting**: it would outlive the moment and come back on the next morning's frame.
- **A dedicated radio button in the viewer bar**: the « … » menu already holds the photo actions, and covers the slideshow too.
- **Reloading the daily lot from an effect keyed on the feed**: Biome flags a dependency the effect body does not read, and a `biome-ignore` has no precedent in the repo.
- **An « empty library » shortcut in `useSourcePhotos`**: it flashed the empty screen before the lot arrived.

## Out of scope

Choosing N or the radio length; keeping a radio for later or sharing it; a « shuffle again » button for the day; selections across accounts; the shooting date (EXIF is stripped); a per-account (rather than per-device) source.

## Open questions (defaults applied meanwhile)

1. **N = 20 and radio length 30** (default). Alternative: a setting, or a size relative to the library.
2. **The day is Paris time for every account** (default). Alternative: a per-account time zone, or the device's (but then devices could disagree).
3. **A photo received before today and opened today counts at its reception date** (known limit, R-CAD-86): it can take a place in the current lot mid-day. A fix needs an `openedAt` field (schema change, `db:push`). **Resolved 2026-11-25 (final review I-1):** `Photo.openedAt` added, see the « Opening date » decision.
4. **Own uploads are not « same sender »** (default). Alternative: count them when the start photo is also an own upload, with a lower weight.
5. **Close dates = at most 7 days apart** (default). Alternative: same calendar day, or a decreasing weight with the gap.
6. **« Aléatoire » also shuffles the radio** (default), so its context order is only kept with « Dans l'ordre ». Alternative: the radio always plays in its order.
7. **A selection or radio that cannot load plays the library silently, with the chosen option still checked** (default, R-CAD-89, R-CAD-97). Alternative: a toast, or the menu showing the library.
8. **The radio is not stored per device**: quitting the frame ends it (default). Alternative: store it like a source until another choice.
9. **No dedicated radio button in the viewer bar**: only the « … » menu (default).
10. **A fourth Socket.IO connection on the frame** while the day's selection plays (feed, mail, collections, daily) (default, same trade-off as step 10's open question 4). Alternative: one shared socket per tab.
11. **A re-drawn lot can raise a false « nouvelle photo » badge**: `useFrameSelection` treats a newer head of the played list as an arrival; a deletion's replacement or the midnight lot can be newer than the shown head (rare; the badge only shows while someone touches the frame). Alternative: detect arrivals on the feed's head, a change to the step 8 hook.
12. **A loaded selection whose photos all left the feed shows « En attente de la première photo… »** even if the library has other photos (rare: the daily lot reloads on `photo:deleted`; a radio whose 30 photos were all deleted). Alternative: play the library in that case.
