# Step 8: frame mode

- Date: 2026-11-25
- Scope: step 8 « Mode cadre ». Frame mode goes full screen, shows photos in display templates, plays in order or shuffled, and remembers its settings per device. Client only: **no API change, no Bruno change, no server change, no new dependency**.
- AI usage: the author asked the AI to design and deliver steps 8 to 11 without waiting for a design session (« je veux que tu livres 8 à 11 sans m'attendre, c'est tout l'intérêt des PR stackées », 2026-11-25). Every decision below was taken by the AI from the goal's brief, with its reason; the author reviews them afterwards on the draft PRs. The ones most worth a second look are listed under Open questions.
- Builds on: `2026-11-24-photo-experience-design.md` (`/frame` is the slideshow only, `useLeaveFrame`, `startFrame`, `FrameStatusScreen`), `2026-11-23-frame-slideshow-design.md` (`useSlideshow`, `useSlideshowSettings`, `SlideshowMenu`, Ken Burns), `2026-11-24-account-settings-design.md` (`/settings` page and its cards).

## Goal

A tablet on a shelf becomes a photo frame with one tap: the browser goes full screen, the photos fill it in the chosen style, in order or shuffled, and the tablet keeps its own choices from one day to the next. Leaving full screen leaves frame mode, by ✕ or by Escape.

## Brief (from the goal)

- Entering frame mode switches the browser to full screen (Fullscreen API, on the click); ✕ leaves full screen and frame mode; Escape too.
- Display templates: full bleed, passe-partout, polaroid (+ proposals to validate in the spec).
- Playback: in order, looping, or shuffled.
- Settings per device (stored in the browser), editable in frame mode and shown again in Préférences.

| Entering full screen              | the three ways in (library « Mode cadre » pill, viewer ▶, app bar « Cadre » and its mobile entry) call `enterFullscreen()` **in their click handler**, then navigate as today                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | browsers only grant full screen during a user gesture; an SPA navigation keeps the same document, so full screen survives the route change                                               |
| `enterFullscreen()`               | `document.documentElement.requestFullscreen()` when `document.fullscreenEnabled`, rejection caught and ignored (`navigationUI: 'hide'`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | iPhone Safari has no element full screen: frame mode still works there, just inside the browser                                                                                          |
| Frame opened without a click      | a frame that boots straight on `/frame` cannot go full screen by itself; the top bar shows a « Plein écran » button (⛶) while not full screen and full screen is available                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | no gesture, no full screen: one tap on the wall frame does it                                                                                                                            |
| What « frame mode » is            | while on `/frame`, full screen and frame mode go together: **leaving full screen by any means** (Escape, F11, the system gesture, ✕) leaves frame mode, through the existing `useLeaveFrame`                                                                                                                                                                                                    | the brief: ✕ and Escape both leave both. The browser eats Escape in full screen (the page never gets the key), so the only reliable signal is `fullscreenchange`                         |
| ✕                                 | leaves frame mode directly, as today; `FramePage`'s unmount then exits full screen (after removing its `fullscreenchange` listener, so nothing navigates twice)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | one path that also works where full screen is unavailable; `CloseButton` stays unchanged                                                                                                 |
| Escape not in full screen         | unchanged: `useEscapeToClose` leaves frame mode                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | resolves step 7's open M7: Escape leaves frame mode in both states                                                                                                                       |
| Leaving `/frame` otherwise        | `FramePage` exits full screen on unmount (back button, app bar link)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | full screen belongs to frame mode only; the library must never stay full screen                                                                                                          |
| Full screen on the status screens | the same: leaving full screen on « Chargement des photos… », the empty or the failed screen goes back to the library                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | one rule for the whole of `/frame`                                                                                                                                                       |
| Templates                         | four: **Ajusté** (default, today's look: whole photo on its blurred backdrop, Ken Burns), **Plein format** (`object-cover`, edge to edge, cropped, Ken Burns), **Passe-partout** (the whole photo inside a wide off-white mat with a thin inner bevel, on a dark wall), **Polaroïd** (a white print with a thick bottom band carrying the date and the sender, slightly tilted, on a dark wall) | the brief's three, plus today's look kept as the default: changing the frame's default look on update day would surprise everyone. That fourth one is the « proposal » the brief invites |
| Ken Burns per template            | on for Ajusté and Plein format; off for Passe-partout and Polaroïd                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | a print zooming inside its mat looks broken; the mat is the still frame                                                                                                                  |
| Caption per template              | Ajusté, Plein format, Passe-partout keep the caption overlay at the bottom (date, « Envoyée par »); Polaroïd writes it in the print's band instead, and hides the overlay                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | a polaroid has its own place for a caption                                                                                                                                               |
| Tilt                              | Polaroïd tilts −1.5° or +1.5° per photo, chosen from the photo's id (not its position, which shifts when a photo arrives)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | a tiny tilt reads « print »; the same photo always keeps the same tilt                                                                                                                   |
| Playback order                    | **Dans l'ordre** (default: newest to oldest, looping, today) and **Aléatoire** (a shuffled order of the loaded photos, drawn once when frame mode starts, reshuffled after a full round)                                                                                                                                                                                                        | the brief; reshuffling per round avoids the same sequence every day                                                                                                                      |
| New photo in shuffle              | an arriving photo goes right after the one on screen, so the existing « a new photo shows at once » rule (R-CAD-2x) keeps working                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | the arrival rule stays true in both orders                                                                                                                                               |
| Deleted photo in shuffle          | removed from the shuffled order; what shows next follows the existing deletion rule (R-CAD-29), in both orders                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | one deletion rule whatever the order                                                                                                                                                     |
| Speed                             | unchanged choices (5, 10, 20 s), now remembered with the other settings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | it was lost at every reload: a wall frame reboots                                                                                                                                        |
| Strip                             | the thumbnail strip toggle stays session-only, not remembered                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | it is a browsing aid for whoever stands in front, not a frame setting                                                                                                                    |
| Storage                           | one `localStorage` key `familyframe.frame-settings` holding `{ template, order, intervalMs }`, read and validated at startup (unknown or missing values fall back to the defaults, no cast), written on each change; every read/write in `try/catch`                                                                                                                                            | per device, as the brief says; a private window or blocked storage must still show a working frame with the defaults                                                                     |
| Settings hook                     | `useFrameSettings()` replaces the state inside `useSlideshowSettings` for those three values; a `storage` event listener keeps two tabs of the same browser in step                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Préférences and the frame can be open side by side on a computer                                                                                                                         |
| In frame mode                     | the ⋯ menu gains two submenus next to « Vitesse »: « Affichage » (the four templates) and « Lecture » (« Dans l'ordre », « Aléatoire »), radio items like « Vitesse »                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | where the speed already is; no new control on the photo                                                                                                                                  |
| In Préférences                    | a fifth card « Cadre de cet appareil »: the same three settings as radio groups, saved at once (no « Enregistrer »), with the note « Ces réglages ne concernent que cet appareil. »                                                                                                                                                                                                             | the brief: shown again in Préférences; local settings need no save round-trip                                                                                                            |
| Status screens                    | unchanged texts; they also get the ⛶ button when not full screen                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | a frame stuck on « Chargement… » can still be made full screen                                                                                                                           |
| Product docs                      | `cadre.md` new rules (full screen, templates, order, per-device settings), `preferences.md` the new card, `a-venir.md` loses « Étape 8 »; R-CAD rules changed by this step marked « Remplacée »                                                                                                                                                                                                 | the docs rule; ids are never reused                                                                                                                                                      |

Rejected: a dedicated full-screen route (`/frame` is already the frame), keeping full screen when leaving frame mode (a library in full screen hides the browser's own back button), a « Plein écran » toggle that leaves full screen but stays in frame mode (the brief ties both together), storing the settings on the account (the brief says per device: a kitchen tablet and a living-room TV want different looks), a random order recomputed at each photo (a photo could come back twice in a row), animating the polaroid tilt (motion for nothing on a wall).

## Client

### PR A: full screen (`feat/frame-fullscreen`)

- `client/src/fullscreen.ts`: `isFullscreenAvailable()`, `isFullscreen()`, `enterFullscreen()`, `exitFullscreen()`; promises caught (a refused request is not an error for the user).
- The three entries call `enterFullscreen()` on click: `LibrarySection`'s pill, `useViewerParam.startFrame`, the nav item « Cadre » (desktop and mobile).
- `useFrameFullscreen(onExit)`: listens to `fullscreenchange` and calls `onExit` when full screen ends while it was on; on unmount it removes the listener, then exits full screen. Used through a `useFrameMode` hook extracted from `FramePage`.
- `CloseButton` unchanged: ✕ leaves, the unmount exits full screen.
- `FullscreenButton` (⛶, « Plein écran », glass style, 44 px) in `SlideshowTopBar` and on `FrameStatusScreen`, rendered only when available and not already full screen.

### PR B: settings remembered per device, in order or shuffled (`feat/frame-playback`)

- `client/src/frameSettings.ts`: types `FrameTemplate = 'fit' | 'fill' | 'mat' | 'polaroid'`, `PlaybackOrder = 'newest' | 'shuffle'`, `FrameSettings`, `DEFAULT_FRAME_SETTINGS`, `parseFrameSettings(raw: unknown): FrameSettings`, `readFrameSettings()`, `writeFrameSettings()`.
- `useFrameSettings()`: state from `readFrameSettings()`, setters write through, `storage` event sync.
- `useSlideshowSettings` keeps `isPlaying` and `isStripVisible` as state and takes the rest from `useFrameSettings`.
- `shuffleOrder.ts`: `shuffledIds(ids, random)`, `insertAfter(order, currentId, newId)`; `usePlaybackPhotos(photos, order)` gives the slideshow its photos in playback order (a stable shuffle, reshuffled when the round wraps).
- `SlideshowMenu`: « Lecture » submenu.

### PR C: display templates (`feat/frame-templates`)

- `PhotoSlide` takes the template; `templateClasses.ts` maps each template to its classes; `PolaroidPrint` renders the print and its band; `PhotoView` hides the backdrop for Plein format / Passe-partout / Polaroïd and the caption overlay for Polaroïd.
- `SlideshowMenu`: « Affichage » submenu.

### PR D: the Préférences card (`feat/settings-frame-card`)

- `components/settings/FrameSettingsCard.tsx` in `SettingsPage`, three single-choice `ToggleGroup`s (no shadcn `radio-group` is installed; Radix renders them as a radio group; no shadcn CLI run). Rules R-PRF-50..52 in a new « Cadre de cet appareil » section of `preferences.md`.

### UI text (French, exact)

- « Plein écran » (button label and `aria-label`).
- Menu: « Affichage », « Ajusté », « Plein format », « Passe-partout », « Polaroïd »; « Lecture », « Dans l'ordre », « Aléatoire »; « Vitesse » unchanged.
- Préférences: card title « Cadre de cet appareil », description « Ces réglages ne concernent que cet appareil. », group labels « Affichage », « Lecture », « Vitesse », speed items « 5 s », « 10 s », « 20 s ».

## Out of scope

Settings stored on the account, a remote control of one frame from another device, transitions other than the current glide, a clock or weather on the frame, a night mode or schedule, playing a collection (step 10) or a selection (step 11) — those steps plug into the playback order added here.
